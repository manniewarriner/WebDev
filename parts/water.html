01 The pure-water difference

Why my windows stay cleaner for longer

Tap water leaves minerals behind, and that's what causes spots and streaks. I use a water-fed pole with ultra-pure water, filtered down to zero dissolved solids, so your glass dries naturally to a perfect, spot-free shine. No ladders, no chemicals, no residue.

  1. 01

    Tap water

    Straight from the tap, Lincolnshire's hard water carries dissolved minerals, often 300 parts per million or more. Wash a window with it and they dry onto the glass as spots and smears.

  2. 02

    Multi-stage filtration

    The water is pushed through reverse osmosis and de-ionising resin, which strip out the calcium, magnesium and salts, particle by particle.

  3. 03

    Ultra-pure: 0 ppm

    Checked with a TDS meter, it comes out at zero dissolved solids, and pure water like this lifts grime off glass and frames without a drop of soap.

  4. 04

    Spot-free finish

    Brushed and rinsed with a water-fed pole from the ground, your windows dry naturally, streak-free, with nothing left behind to attract new dirt.
