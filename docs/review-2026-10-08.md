## A. The big one: the "template" look

The frontend-design guidance lists traits that make a site read as AI-generated. Our redesign uses several of them, and they came straight from `docs/redesign.md`:
- all-caps monospace labels such as "02 — WHAT I DO"
- 01/02/03 numbers on things that aren't steps (the services, FAQ and area list)
- one italic coloured word in each headline ("*clearest*", "*proof*", "*instant*")
- thin rules and corner ticks on every block

Each one is fine on its own, but together they make the site look like a template, not like Lincoln's own window cleaner.

| # | Suggestion | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Replace the all-caps mono labels with short sentence-case labels in the main font, or remove them where the heading already says it. | S |
| A2 | Keep the numbers only where they show real steps (the 4 pure-water steps). Remove them from services, FAQ, areas and section labels. | S |
| A3 | Use one headline accent across the whole site (for example, only the hero's "clearest"), not one per section. | S |
| A4 | Pick one framing device per section: either the hairline rules or the corner ticks, not both. | S |
