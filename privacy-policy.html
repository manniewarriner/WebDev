Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Introduction

Lincoln Window Cleaner ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name: When you request a quote or contact us
  • Phone number: For appointment scheduling and communication
  • Address: To identify your property and provide service
  • Messages: Any communications you send via WhatsApp, phone, or our contact form

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our window cleaning services
  • Process and fulfill your quotes and service requests
  • Communicate with you about appointments and service details
  • Send you important updates about your bookings
  • Respond to your inquiries and messages
  • Comply with legal obligations

Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under UK GDPR:

  • Contract performance: To provide cleaning services you've requested or quoted for
  • Legal obligation: To comply with tax, insurance, and other UK legal requirements
  • Legitimate interest: To operate our business efficiently and communicate about services

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Typically:

  • Contact information for active customers is kept while the service relationship exists
  • Quote requests are retained for 30 days if no booking is made
  • Records required for tax purposes are kept for 6 years
  • After the end of our relationship, we delete your data unless we're required to keep it by law

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to others. We may share information only with:

  • Your explicit consent
  • Legal authorities when required by law
  • Insurance providers (limited to essential details for coverage)

Website Cookies and Tracking

This website uses no tracking cookies. We do load external resources from:

  • Google Fonts: For typography
  • Google Maps: The coverage map on our homepage is embedded from Google Maps, which may set its own cookies
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare): For JavaScript libraries
  • unpkg: For JavaScript libraries

These services may set their own cookies for performance and analytics, but we do not set or read tracking cookies ourselves.

The Google map only loads after you choose Accept in the cookie banner or press Show the map. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (key lwc-consent). To change it, clear this site's data in your browser and the banner will appear again.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data (where applicable)
  • Object to processing of your information
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Request your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

Data Protection Officer

As a small sole-trader business, we are committed to protecting your privacy. Our customers can exercise their data protection rights at any time by contacting us directly on the number above.
